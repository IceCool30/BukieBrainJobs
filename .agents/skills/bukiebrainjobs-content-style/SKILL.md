# BukieBrainJobs Content Style Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical Standard  
**Scope:** Customer-facing platform content, marketplace terminology, and microcopy  
**Authority:** Governs BukieBrainJobs customer-facing content and screen copy alongside `mr-solomon-natural-voice`.

---

## 1. Core Principles

### 1.1 Voice and Tone
Always use:
- **Active voice:** "We protect your information."
- **Direct address:** Use "You" and "We" where appropriate.
- **Confident and clear:** State what we know without unnecessary hedging.
- **Professional and approachable:** Sound credible, useful, and human.
- **Action-oriented:** Tell users what they can do next.
- **Benefit-focused:** Explain why an action or feature matters.

Avoid:
- Passive voice where active voice is clearer.
- Third-person references such as "the user."
- Overly casual language.
- Apologetic language that weakens confidence.
- Empty marketing language.
- Claims we cannot verify or support.

---

## 2. Language and Terminology

Consistent terminology is mandatory across all customer-facing content.

### 2.1 Approved Marketplace Terms

| Concept | Preferred Term | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Person hiring | Customer | Client, Employer |
| Service provider | BrainWorker | Worker, Artisan, Talent |
| Generic service provider reference | Professional | Worker, Artisan |
| Work request | Job | Gig, Task |
| Service offering | Service | Gig |
| Verification process | Verification | Vetting (internal use only) |
| Escrow payment | Secure Payment / Escrow | Holding account |
| Payment release | Milestone Release | Payout |

---

## 3. Formatting & Grammar Standards

- **Punctuation:** Never use em dashes (`—` or `--`). Use commas, colons, or clean sentence breaks.
- **Currency:** Use canonical Nigerian Naira formatting: `₦` with comma separation (e.g., `₦2,800,000`).
- **Dates & Times:** Use clear, unambiguous formatting (e.g., `Oct 9, 2026`).
- **Buttons & Calls to Action:** Use direct, action-oriented verbs: `Book BrainWorker`, `Post Requisition`, `View Profile`, `Confirm Booking`.
