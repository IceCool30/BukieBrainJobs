# BukieBrainJobs Roadmap

**Document ID:** PROD-002
**Version:** 1.1
**Status:** Planning baseline

This roadmap organizes the work into product milestones. It does not authorize implementation of every future feature immediately.

## Milestone 1: Foundation

Status: Complete at planning level

Includes:

- Product foundation
- Marketplace research
- Technical specification
- Design System v1.0
- Operating workflow
- Repository governance

## Milestone 2: Public Website

Status: In progress

Core areas for first public launch:

- Homepage
- Service categories
- Search and discovery
- Service details
- BrainWorker profiles
- About / trust pages (including BukieGuarantee)
- Help and contact entry points
- Authentication entry points

Public signal only (not launch capability):

- `/enterprise` Coming Soon page for future business solutions

Current approved features:

- `WEB-001` Homepage
- `WEB-002` Enterprise route kept as Coming Soon (not launch scope)

## Milestone 3: Authentication

Planned:

- Login
- Registration
- OTP
- Verification
- Password recovery
- Role-aware routing
- Session management

## Milestone 4: Customer Platform

Planned:

- Dashboard
- Search and discovery
- Job posting
- Matching
- Booking
- Payments
- Reviews
- Wallet
- Notifications
- Profile and settings

## Milestone 5: BrainWorker Platform

Planned:

- Onboarding
- Identity and professional verification
- Profile
- Skills
- Rates
- Availability
- Job management
- Earnings
- Portfolio
- Performance

## Milestone 6: Booking and Payments

Planned:

- Job lifecycle
- Scheduling
- Check-in
- Completion
- Payment authorization
- Split payments
- Payouts
- Refunds
- Disputes

## Milestone 7: Corporate Portal

Status: Post-launch planning only

Planned after the consumer marketplace is live:

- Organization management
- Team management
- Recurring services
- Billing
- Reporting

Public website note: `/enterprise` remains a Coming Soon signal until this milestone is approved for implementation.

## Milestone 8: Admin Platform

Planned:

- Operations
- User management
- Verification
- Jobs
- Payments
- Disputes
- Content management
- Analytics

## Milestone 9: AI Features

Future capabilities will be evaluated against product value, cost, reliability, privacy and operational benefit before implementation.

## Milestone 10: Production Readiness

Includes:

- Full QA
- Security review
- Accessibility review
- Performance review
- Observability
- Backup and recovery
- Deployment validation
- Launch readiness

## Milestone 11: Launch

Includes:

- Production deployment
- App Store and Play Store readiness
- Website readiness for the consumer marketplace
- Analytics verification
- Payment verification
- Customer support readiness
- Rollback plan
- Post-launch monitoring

Launch does not include Corporate Portal operations. Business solutions stay Coming Soon until Milestone 7 is approved and built.

## Sequencing rule

A milestone can contain work that is designed but not yet implemented. Implementation should follow the approved feature workflow and current product priorities.
