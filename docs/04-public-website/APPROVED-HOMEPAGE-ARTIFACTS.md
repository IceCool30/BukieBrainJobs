## Locked homepage decisions

### Audience

Customers are the primary audience. BrainWorker acquisition is a strong secondary pathway.

### Primary action

**Search for a Service** is the primary homepage conversion.

### Three marketplace entry paths

1. Find and book a BrainWorker.
2. Post a job and get matched.
3. Become a BrainWorker.

The visual hierarchy must keep search primary while making the other two paths discoverable.

### Discovery

Guests may browse services, locations, BrainWorker profiles, reviews and public pricing without creating an account.

### Search

The homepage search starts with service and location. Date and time belong later in the booking journey.

### Geography

The initial geographic architecture covers 36 Nigerian state capitals plus Abuja, FCT. Marketplace availability uses controlled activation. An unactivated capital must be presented as coming soon rather than falsely shown as live.

### Trust

Public trust language may communicate identity verification, professional verification, secure payments, transparent pricing, platform protection and support. Sensitive verification data such as NIN, BVN and government identification numbers must never appear publicly.

### Customer trust

The customer trust model is risk-based. Discovery remains low friction, while stronger verification may be required for higher-risk actions.

## Responsive baseline

- Mobile: 4-column grid.
- Tablet: 8-column grid.
- Desktop: 12-column grid.
- Maximum content width: 1280px.
- Standard gutter: 24px.

Layouts must be recomposed responsively, not merely scaled down.

## Content integrity

Production testimonials and metrics must come from real data. If real records do not exist, use clearly illustrative placeholders during design and never imply that fictional content represents real customers.

## Design guardrails

Do not:

- Turn the homepage into a dashboard.
- Force registration before discovery.
- Claim nationwide live availability without activation.
- Expose sensitive verification information.
- Add map functionality to the hero unless an approved specification changes.
- Overload the hero with fields.
- Make all three entry paths equally dominant.
- Use generic stock imagery as a substitute for the approved visual direction.
- Invent new design tokens.
