## Locked Product Decisions

- Primary audience: customers first.
- Primary CTA: Search for a Service.
- Hero discovery: service + location.
- Guest browsing: allowed.
- Booking authentication: required at commitment point.
- Marketplace paths: find and book, post a job and get matched, become a BrainWorker.
- Geographic architecture: 36 Nigerian state capitals plus Abuja, FCT.
- Market activation: controlled by marketplace supply and coverage thresholds.
- Search results: real BrainWorkers should be discoverable quickly.
- Trust signals: visible throughout discovery.
- Mobile-first experience.
- No map-led hero.
- No registration wall before discovery.

## Design Guardrails

- `DESIGN.md` is the visual authority.
- Deep Navy is primary.
- Emerald remains strategic and must not dominate the interface.
- Do not invent foundational tokens.
- Do not expose sensitive verification information.
- Do not turn the homepage into a dashboard.
- Do not treat all three marketplace paths as equal to the primary search CTA.
- Use authentic Nigerian imagery where imagery is required.
- Respect WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Engineering Boundary

Antigravity must not implement WEB-001C until the Stitch design has been reviewed and explicitly approved by the project owner.
